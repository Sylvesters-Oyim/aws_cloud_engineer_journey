Well-Architected, Support Plans and Week 2 Review

By the end of today you can Judge any design against the six Well-Architected pillars, pick the right AWS Support plan, and pass a 15-question Week 2 mock.

1. The Well-Architected Framework

AWS distilled years of customer mistakes into six questions to ask about any system. Memorise them; they appear on both exams.

Analogy: inspecting a house before buyingStrong foundations (reliability), good locks (security), cheap bills (cost), fast lifts (performance), easy maintenance (operational excellence), and a small environmental footprint (sustainability).
PillarKey questionExample tactic
Operational excellenceCan we run and improve it?Infrastructure as code, runbooks, small frequent changes
SecurityIs it protected?Least privilege, MFA, encrypt everything
ReliabilityDoes it recover from failure?Multi-AZ, backups, auto scaling
Performance efficiencyAre we using the right resources?Right instance type, CDN, serverless
Cost optimizationAre we avoiding waste?Right-sizing, Savings Plans, turn off idle
SustainabilityAre we minimising impact?Efficient hardware, higher utilisation

Also know the 6 advantages of cloud (exam): trade fixed for variable expense; benefit from massive economies of scale; stop guessing capacity; increase speed and agility; stop spending money running data centres; go global in minutes.

4. Week 2 mock exam (15 questions, aim for 12+)

1. A subnet is public because its route table has a route to ____.An Internet Gateway.
2. Stateless firewall at subnet level that supports deny rules?Network ACL.
3. Private instances need outbound internet only. Use?NAT Gateway.
4. Which record type maps the zone apex to a CloudFront distribution?Alias.
5. Route users to the lowest-latency Region?Route 53 latency-based routing.
6. Standby website that takes over automatically?Failover routing + health check.
7. Reduce latency for static assets worldwide?CloudFront.
8. Keep S3 private behind CloudFront?Origin Access Control.
9. Service that records API calls for auditing?CloudTrail.
10. Managed DDoS protection included at no cost?AWS Shield Standard.
11. Free way to reach S3 from a private subnet without NAT?S3 gateway VPC endpoint.
12. Who is responsible for patching guest OS on EC2?The customer.
13. Which support plan first includes 24/7 phone/chat access?Business (Developer is business-hours email only).
14. Which Well-Architected pillar covers multi-AZ and backups?Reliability.
15. Tool to estimate a deployment's monthly cost before building?AWS Pricing Calculator.

Score below 12? Re-do the lab for the days where you missed answers; reading again won't fix it.
