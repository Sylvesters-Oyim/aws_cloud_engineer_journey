AWS Cloud Engineer Journey

A hands-on path from zero to AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner, then Solutions Architect – Associate, built around real labs you run in your own account. Every day has: a first-principles explanation, a real-life analogy, a diagram, a lab, and an exam-style quiz.

How the course works
  • Weekdays: 1–2 hours = one module. Weekends: up to 4 hours = a bigger lab + review.
  • Rule of thumb: 30% reading, 70% hands-on. If you only read, it won't stick.
  • Every lab ends with a cleanup step so you don't get a surprise bill.
  • Drop your own console screenshots into evidence/ (see each lab). That's your portfolio.
